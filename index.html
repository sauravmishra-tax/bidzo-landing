AI listing report

Drop in an ASIN, get back exactly what to fix

Our AI reads the live listing the way a shopper and Amazon's search algorithm both do, then scores it against the top-ranking competitors in that category — no generic checklist.

What the report covers

  • → Title: keyword placement, length, and readability vs. top 10 competitors
  • → Bullets & description: missing benefit claims, search terms, and compliance risks
  • → Images & A+ content: gaps against category norms (lifestyle shots, comparison charts, infographics)
  • → Backend search terms & indexing: keywords you rank for vs. keywords you're missing

How you get it

Send us the ASIN and we'll run it through the model and a manual sanity check, then send back a prioritized report — ranked by expected impact on conversion, not just a list of nitpicks.
