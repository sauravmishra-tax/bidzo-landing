AaTitle

  • → Main keyword appears in the first 60–80 characters.
  • → Includes brand, key attribute (size/color/count), and use-case — not just keyword stuffing.
  • → Under Amazon's category character limit, no ALL-CAPS or banned symbols.
  • → Readable to a human, not just crawlable by search.

¶Bullets & description

  • → Each bullet leads with a benefit, not just a spec.
  • → Top 5–8 secondary keywords spread naturally across bullets.
  • → Addresses the 2–3 most common objections seen in competitor reviews.
  • → No unverifiable claims that could trigger a compliance flag.

▦Images & A+ content

  • → At least 6–7 images: hero shot, in-use/lifestyle, size/scale, infographic, comparison chart.
  • → Main image meets Amazon's pure-white-background requirement.
  • → A+ content covers brand story, use-cases, and a comparison table if you sell variants.
  • → Text overlays are legible on mobile thumbnails.

#Backend keywords & indexing

  • → Backend search terms field is filled to the byte limit, no repeats of title words.
  • → Includes spelling variants, synonyms, and regional terms not already in the title/bullets.
  • → Check indexing for your top 10 target keywords (search + look for your ASIN).

★Reviews & trust signals

  • → Star rating and review count are visible above the fold on mobile.
  • → Recent negative reviews are read for recurring listing-fixable complaints (sizing, packaging, expectations set by images).
  • → Q&A section has no unanswered questions that block conversion.
